Apply from the root of Integrated-OpenCV-Farming-Agent:

cp pybullet_sim/gantry_robot.py pybullet_sim/gantry_robot.py.bak
cp pybullet_sim/bridge.py pybullet_sim/bridge.py.bak
cp farm_engine.py farm_engine.py.bak

Then extract this archive into the project root, preserving paths. The archive contains:
  pybullet_sim/gantry_robot.py
  pybullet_sim/bridge.py
  farm_engine.py
  MULTIVIEW_OPERATIONAL_UPGRADE.md

The project root is the folder containing app_ui.py and farm_engine.py. This patch assumes
that the prior multi-view localization and vertical-plant patch are already installed.
